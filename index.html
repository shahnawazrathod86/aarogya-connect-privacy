Privacy Policy

Aarogya Connect (Android app) · Publisher: A3S DIGITECH · Last updated: 19 September 2026

In plain language: your health records stay encrypted on your device; there is no account and no cloud copy. Location is used only when you ask for "near me" results. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, and you can delete everything the app stores in one tap.

1. What we store on your device

Aarogya Connect keeps your health records — blood pressure, blood glucose, vaccination history, pregnancy (ANC/PNC) checkups, TB medicine adherence, menstrual cycle entries, medication reminders, BMI calculations, saved facilities, family member profiles and quiz results — only on this device.

Every record is encrypted at rest with a 256-bit key that lives only in your device's secure keystore (Android Keystore), never inside the app's own data files.

2. What we do not do

This app has no user account, no cloud database, and no backend server for your personal or health data. Nothing described in section 1 is ever uploaded anywhere by this app. No analytics or tracking SDK runs in this app beyond the advertising SDK described in section 4.

3. Basic profile (name, sex, age, email)

If you choose to enter your name, sex, age or email address — all optional — it is stored on your device the same encrypted way as your health records, used only to personalise the app (for example greeting you by name), and never transmitted off your device by this app.

4. Advertising

Aarogya Connect shows banner ads served through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To select and measure ads, AdMob can collect your device's advertising identifier, general device information (model, OS version, language, country), coarse location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data (impressions, clicks). This data is collected and processed by Google, not stored by Aarogya Connect.

Your health data is never shared with AdMob or any advertiser. Nothing from section 1 — no health record, reminder or profile field — is ever passed to the ad SDK, an ad request, or any advertising partner.

You can limit ad personalisation on your device: Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google Settings → Ads on older Android versions) lets you reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads. See Google's Partner Sites policy and Privacy Policy.

5. Location and pincode search

When you search for nearby facilities, your pincode or GPS coordinates are sent to map-data services solely to return that one search result: Google Places (Google Maps) and, when Google is unavailable, OpenStreetMap's Nominatim, Photon and Overpass APIs, plus India Post's public pincode directory. Only the coordinates or pincode needed for that search are sent; your name, health data or any other identifying information is never included. Location access is requested only when you tap "near me", never at launch, and searching by pincode is always offered as an alternative that needs no location permission.

6. Notifications

Medication and vaccination reminders are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device using Android's own notification system. No reminder content is sent through, or generated by, any external push-notification server.

7. Children's information

Aarogya Connect is a general-audience app and is not directed at, or knowingly used to collect information from, children under 13. The Child Growth feature is a tool for a parent or guardian to record their own child's growth measurements; that data is entered by the adult user, stored on the device under the same encryption as every other health record, and never transmitted anywhere.

8. Your control over your data

You can review what is stored, and permanently delete all of it, at any time from Profile → My data → Delete all my data. Deletion is immediate and on the device: every health record, reminder and profile field is wiped, scheduled reminders are cancelled, and the app reopens as if freshly installed. We cannot undo this because we never had a copy.

10. Medical disclaimer

Aarogya Connect provides general educational information and personal record-keeping tools. It is not a diagnosis or treatment and does not replace a qualified healthcare professional. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
