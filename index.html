Privacy Policy

Aarogya Connect (Android app) · Publisher: A3S DIGITECH · Last updated: 24 September 2026

In plain language: your health records stay encrypted on your device; there is no account and no cloud copy. Location is used only when you ask for "near me" results. The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Our AI helper, AASHU, can use Google Gemini only if you switch it on, and it never sees your saved records. You can delete everything the app stores in one tap.

1. What we store on your device

Aarogya Connect keeps your health records — blood pressure, blood glucose, vaccination history, pregnancy (ANC/PNC) checkups, TB medicine adherence, menstrual cycle entries, medication reminders, BMI calculations, daily step counts and water-intake entries, saved facilities, family member profiles and quiz results — only on this device.

Every record is encrypted at rest with a 256-bit key that lives only in your device's secure keystore (Android Keystore), never inside the app's own data files.

2. What we do not do

This app has no user account, no cloud database, and no backend server for your personal or health data. Nothing described in section 1 is ever uploaded anywhere by this app. The only things that leave your device are described in sections 4, 5 and 6: ads, map and location searches, and, only if you switch it on, the questions you type to the AASHU AI helper. No analytics or tracking SDK runs in this app beyond the advertising SDK described in section 4.

3. Basic profile (name, sex, age, email)

If you choose to enter your name, sex, age or email address — all optional — it is stored on your device the same encrypted way as your health records, used only to personalise the app (for example greeting you by name), and never transmitted off your device by this app.

4. Advertising

Aarogya Connect shows banner ads served through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To select and measure ads, AdMob can collect your device's advertising identifier, general device information (model, OS version, language, country), coarse location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data (impressions, clicks). This data is collected and processed by Google, not stored by Aarogya Connect.

Your health data is never shared with AdMob or any advertiser. Nothing from section 1 — no health record, reminder or profile field — is ever passed to the ad SDK, an ad request, or any advertising partner.

You can limit ad personalisation on your device: Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google Settings → Ads on older Android versions) lets you reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads. See Google's Partner Sites policy and Privacy Policy.

5. Location and pincode search

When you search for nearby facilities, your pincode or GPS coordinates and the kind of place you searched for are sent to map-data services solely to return that one search result: Google Maps data (through the Google Gemini API) and, when Google is unavailable, OpenStreetMap's Nominatim, Photon and Overpass APIs, plus India Post's public pincode directory. To show approximate distances, the address of each result may be looked up by your phone's own location service, which is provided by Google Play services. Only the coordinates, pincode or address needed for that search is sent; your name, health data or any other identifying information is never included. Location access is requested only when you tap "near me", never at launch, and searching by pincode is always offered as an alternative that needs no location permission.

6. AASHU, the AI helper, and Google Gemini

AASHU answers health questions in two ways, and always tells you which one wrote each answer:

  • Built-in guide — fixed, checked answers that work entirely inside the app. It sends nothing anywhere.
  • Google Gemini AI — used only if you choose "Use Google Gemini AI" after reading a clear notice. You can switch it off at any time on the AASHU screen.

When Gemini is on, the question you type and your last few chat messages are sent to Google Gemini so it can write an answer. Nothing else is sent — never your saved health records, name, age, email or location.

Please do not type personal details (names, phone numbers, medical reports) into the chat. On Google's free Gemini service, Google may use what is sent to it to improve its products. Google handles that data under Google's Privacy Policy and the Gemini API terms.

Emergency, crisis and other high-risk topics are never sent to Gemini; the app answers those itself with fixed guidance and the 108/112 emergency numbers. If Gemini is unavailable or its free limit is reached, the built-in guide answers instead. Chat messages are kept only on your screen and disappear when you leave the chat; the app does not save them.

AASHU is an AI, not a doctor. Its answers, especially those written by Gemini, can contain mistakes. They are general educational information, not a diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional, and call 108 or 112 in an emergency.

7. Notifications

Medication and vaccination reminders are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device using Android's own notification system. No reminder content is sent through, or generated by, any external push-notification server.

8. Step counting (physical activity)

Step counting is optional. If you turn it on, Android asks for the "Physical activity" (activity recognition) permission. Aarogya Connect then reads your phone's own step counter to show today's steps, and estimates distance, calories and active minutes from those steps and, if you entered them, your height and weight. Steps and the water glasses you log are stored only on this device, in the same encrypted storage as your other health records. They are never uploaded, shared with advertisers, or sent to Google Gemini or any other service. Distance and calories are estimates, not medical measurements. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone's Settings; the app keeps working and you can enter steps by hand. "Delete all my data" in the app also erases your step and water history.

9. Children's information

Aarogya Connect is a general-audience app and is not directed at, or knowingly used to collect information from, children under 13. The Child Growth feature is a tool for a parent or guardian to record their own child's growth measurements; that data is entered by the adult user, stored on the device under the same encryption as every other health record, and never transmitted anywhere.

10. Your control over your data

You can review what is stored, and permanently delete all of it, at any time from Profile → My data → Delete all my data. Deletion is immediate and on the device: every health record, reminder and profile field is wiped, scheduled reminders are cancelled, and the app reopens as if freshly installed. We cannot undo this because we never had a copy. You can also switch Google Gemini off at any time on the AASHU screen.
